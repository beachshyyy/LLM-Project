
# Receipt Analysis Guide

## DOC-001: Receipt Amount Terminology

Common amount labels found on receipts:

- TOTAL: Usually refers to the final amount payable or paid.
- GRAND TOTAL: Usually refers to the final combined amount.
- AMOUNT DUE: The amount that remains to be paid.
- NET TOTAL: May refer to the final amount after adjustments.
- SUBTOTAL: Usually refers to the sum before discounts, taxes, or other adjustments.
- DISCOUNT: An amount deducted from the original price.
- VAT / TAX: Tax charged on a transaction.

Important:
The meaning of a label can vary by merchant and receipt format.
Use the surrounding text and numerical relationships to identify
the correct final amount. Do not assume that the largest number
is always the final total.

## DOC-002: Thai Receipt Terminology

Common Thai labels and their possible meanings:

- รวมเงิน: Total amount or sum.
- รวมทั้งสิ้น: Grand total.
- ยอดสุทธิ: Net amount or final amount.
- จำนวนเงินที่ต้องชำระ: Amount due.
- รวมก่อนส่วนลด: Subtotal before discount.
- ส่วนลด: Discount.
- ภาษีมูลค่าเพิ่ม: Value Added Tax (VAT).

Important:
Some receipts display both pre-discount and post-discount amounts.
Check the labels and transaction context before selecting the final total.

## DOC-003: Identifying Purchased Items

When extracting purchased items:

- Include products or services that were actually purchased.
- Distinguish purchased items from promotional messages and coupons.
- A privilege, reward, or redemption entitlement is not necessarily
  a purchased item.
- A zero-price entry may represent a free item, privilege, or promotion.
  Check its description and receipt context before including it.
- Do not invent item names, quantities, or prices when information is missing.

## DOC-004: Handling Discounts and Amount Relationships

Common receipt amount relationships:

- Subtotal is generally the amount before deductions.
- Discount reduces the amount payable.
- Final total is generally the amount after applicable adjustments.

A possible calculation is:

Final Total = Subtotal - Discount + Additional Charges

However, not every receipt follows this exact structure.
Some receipts include tax before or after discounts, service charges,
or other adjustments.

Do not calculate a missing printed total by assuming that all
adjustments are known. Use the printed amount when it is clearly available.
